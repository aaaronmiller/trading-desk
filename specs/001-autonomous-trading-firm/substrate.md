# Substrate Self-Location

## Declared level

S3. The owners direct and judge outcomes; an AI build session develops, tests and verifies against gates and held-out evaluations. The project reaches toward S4 because the owners will not read the code, but it does not claim S4: the held-out evaluations are separated only by procedure (the implementer could open them), and most gates are run by the same harness that writes the code.

## Pre-locked decisions

Fixed at preparation time, which a higher level would resolve live:
- Hosting on the Cloudflare Workers Paid plan with Cron Triggers, Workflows, Durable Objects, D1 and R2 (plan CTX-002 to CTX-004).
- TypeScript on Bun, Hono for the service Worker, SvelteKit with shadcn-svelte for the portal, Zod for contracts, an in-house seat runner (CTX-001, CTX-007).
- The 32-seat roster and its reporting lines (CTX-013); the heat mapping table values (CTX-012).
- Default model route profile and families (CTX-009); default data sources per class (CTX-005).
- The plan language's function whitelist (CTX-008).
- Cadences: 15-minute cycles, hourly analysis, 06:00 Pacific daily report.
- The 25-dollar monthly ceiling and throttle order (CTX-010), pending the owners' answer to intent question 1.

## Live-resolved decisions

Resolved during the build or by the running firm:
- Exact model identifiers, prices and structured-output support per route (verified against providers at build time).
- Reachability, rate limits, fees and response shapes of each data host (first live smoke test; recorded fixtures until then).
- Workflow, Durable Object and D1 limits as measured on the account.
- Prompt wording of every seat, skill text and strategy parameters (tuned only on non-held-out data through validation).
- Seat revisions, retirements and capital allocation (the virtual chief executive, at runtime).
- Detector thresholds for special situations and market integrity (calibrated on recorded cases).

## Empirical memory state

Partial. The ledger carries entries 001 to 016 from the legacy kickoff (decisions only, no deployed outcomes) and the vantage planning record. No prior project in the workspace recorded a deployed outcome for this kind of runtime, so architecture derivation rests on research reports R01 to R16, not on experience.

## Known reach

Claimed but unproven:
- That the firm can earn net positive simulated returns after costs, or approach the 1 to 3 percent per day appetite; the feasibility report exists to measure this honestly.
- That free data sources sustain minute-level crypto and intraday multi-asset operation within their terms.
- That the throttled budget still yields useful judgment quality at about 70 percent of full activity.
- That generated harness files work in all six harnesses beyond schema validation.
- That injection and poisoning defenses hold against adversarial content beyond the test corpus.
- That the platform limits recorded in research hold on the owner's account.
